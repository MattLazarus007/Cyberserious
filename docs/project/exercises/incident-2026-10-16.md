# Benign incident exercise, 16 October 2026

A stopped worker, a waiting queue and a restart, rehearsed against
the lab database. The five-minute warning flag itself is covered by
the automated suite, not by waiting here: the ready-shape test pins
the flag's wiring and T-25 proves expired leases recover.

## What ran

One unevaluated case sat on the outbox with no worker running. The
ready endpoint read pending 1, warn false, which is the correct
reading for a young queue: jobs wait, nothing is lost.

The worker loop ran once. The job leased, the case evaluated, the
job finished. The ready endpoint then read pending 0.

## Recovery reading

No manual queue edits were needed at any point. Leases expire and
return to pending by design, and the sweep reopens expired snoozes
in the same pass.

## What was slow

Nothing in the mechanism. The lesson is operational: nobody watches
the ready endpoint unless watching it is someone's job. The group
roster should name who checks queue age before each demo.
