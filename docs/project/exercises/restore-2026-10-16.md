A timed run of docs/runbooks/restore.md against the lab machine.
Nothing was pushed anywhere. The scratch database was dropped
afterwards.

## What ran

then checksums, then a restore into a fresh cyberserious_restore
database, then reconciliation.

work behind. Recovery time: minutes, not hours, at this data size.
