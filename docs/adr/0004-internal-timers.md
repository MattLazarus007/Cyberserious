# ADR-0004: Internal timers are labelled as internal

Date: 2026-10-16. Owner: Group 1.

The alert rules use waiting periods of four, twenty four and forty
eight hours. These numbers pace the demo. They are not legal
deadlines, and the interface never presents them as such.

POPIA speaks of notifying as soon as reasonably possible. No fixed
hour count appears in the Act. Every screen and message that shows
one of these intervals carries the word internal next to it.

If the group ever adopts different pacing, the values change in one
rules module. No screen text needs editing.
